# Chapter 14 — The Hidden Floor

---

## Setting the Scene

The Calvo Manco's search through the Durst house continues in the Curse of Strahd arc. The children remain beyond the party's reach, and the house has revealed another unsettling secret: it is moving. While Tidus explores a strange version of a bedroom, Nixira and Zacarías must work from the physical room to understand what he has found.

---

## The Story

**The House in Motion**

The travelers revisit what they have learned: the movement outside comes not from racing clouds but from the house itself, carried by its roots. Tidus has found another plane by lying down, and the party has resorted to writing on a wardrobe to communicate across the divide.

Tidus returns to the other side to examine a spherical puzzle. He remembers stories of elaborate objects that, when correctly assembled, produced a wooden key. While he sleeps, Zacarías quietly takes ten gold coins from his pockets.

Outside, rain begins as the house moves toward a valley. Zacarías recognizes a powerful magical storm associated with the erasure of memories. Alarmed, he conceals his religious objects and grimoire and takes precautions against leaving a spiritual trace. The storm's source and full effects remain unknown.

**A Mirror and a Cradle**

Nixira studies the bedrooms' mirrors and realizes that they show different rooms across the divide. Behind one frame lies an older ritual mirror, marked with glyphs and fitted with a circular opening and a place for a key. Through it she can see Tidus and the puzzle he is examining. His attempts to touch the mirror make it ripple, but it remains solid to him.

Zacarías sends his bat to investigate the cradle. Though a swaddled baby appears to lie inside, the bat senses spherical fragments instead. Nixira uncovers the bundle and finds three pieces decorated with parts of a baby's face. She wraps them safely in the blanket.

Despite Tidus's warning not to come, Zacarías lies down and joins him on the other side. There he sees the eyes watching from the ceiling. The little dog Eon proves able to interact with both planes, helping move puzzle pieces to Nixira. As Zacarías tries to explore, a violent force throws him back. His sleeping body coughs blood, and Nixira heals him from the physical room.

Nixira fits the recovered fragments together and adds the jewel taken earlier from the piano. It holds the incomplete sphere together, but a piece is still missing. Tidus notices the scent of fresh tea and a magical trace on his side of the room, another clue with no clear owner.

**A Reading for Zacarías**

While the others investigate, Tidus reads Zacarías's cards. A reversed Wheel of Fortune is interpreted as death fleeing him, with an unusual promise of renewed life should he fall. Another card associates his eventual death with flames, though the meaning need not be literal.

A further draw leaves Zacarías feeling compelled to protect Tidus and give him valuable coins. He hands Tidus two platinum pieces. The full terms of the effect are not clear, but the reading leaves both prophecy and obligation behind it.

Eon draws attention to the tea things. Nixira, sensing the cold of the other plane, asks the dog to bring the others back.

**The Secret Stairs**

A gap in the recording leaves part of the next puzzle unrecorded. The players recount a bathroom puzzle involving water, cups and tea; Tidus and Zacarías persuaded Nixira that urinating into the teapot was part of a ritual. Their recap says that the resulting piece or ball was placed in the mirror frame.

A hidden staircase leads upward toward the children's floor. The way is cloaked in magical darkness that pushes back Nixira's flame. The party hears the children above and hopes to reach them, but on the concealed floor the travelers lose track of one another. Tidus fires a burning crossbow bolt into the dark; it lodges in something unseen.

Zacarías takes Tidus's wolf-head mask and considers using it as a torch, angering its owner. Feeling his way onward, Tidus encounters a metal figure and tries to remove its helmet. The armor strikes him down. After a frightened search, Nixira and Zacarías find him near the armor, its hand bloody. Nixira stabilizes him. She suspects the armor is held still by light, though the party cannot establish the full rules of its movement.

**The Map and the Warnings**

Beyond a door carved with a mill, the party enters a cobwebbed room with a desk and a bed bearing an old impression. There they find a map of a bat-shaped valley, marked with a castle, settlements and a purple line. Its name is Barovia. What had sounded to them like a story now appears to be a real place.

A hand mirror briefly shows Nixira her own skull beneath decaying flesh before her reflection returns to normal. At the back of a wardrobe, the party works out a phrase: *From the sky I see everything.* The writing glows, revealing hundreds of messages in many languages. Their writers plead for escape, warn that others cannot be trusted, insist that Barovia is real and urge readers never to approach the well.

Nixira considers finding a wizard who could take them away. Tidus searches the room and finds a hollow-sounding section of wood. The party has not yet opened it when the search pauses.

## Key Revelations

- **The house moves** — Its roots carry it through the landscape, toward a valley beneath a powerful magical storm.
- **The bedrooms overlap** — Their mirrors connect the travelers' view of rooms on different sides of a divide; Eon can interact with both planes.
- **The cradle holds puzzle pieces** — The apparent infant conceals fragments of a sphere. A jewel fits the incomplete assembly; the players later recount placing a piece or ball in the mirror frame.
- **Tidus's reading has consequences** — The cards promise Zacarías an uncertain reprieve at death, associate his eventual death with flames and impose an obligation toward Tidus.
- **The hidden floor is dangerous** — Magical darkness obscures the upper floor, where armor strikes Tidus down.
- **Barovia appears on the map** — The wardrobe's messages also name it, plead for escape and warn against approaching a well.

---

## Consequences

- **The children remain trapped** — The party has reached the concealed floor but has not freed Rose and Thorn.
- **The armor remains a threat** — Tidus was stabilized after its attack; it has not been shown destroyed.
- **The party has a new warning** — The messages urge distrust and flight, but their authors and the well's location remain unknown.
- **The house still holds secrets** — The hollow wooden section has not been opened, and the travelers have not escaped.
- **The search continues** — The recordings do not establish the whereabouts of Dorotea or Vermilion.
