# Chapter 10 — The Bloodstained Crest

---

## Setting the Scene

After escaping the Catacombs of Bithanu, the Calvo Manco return to La Ultima Gota with a new guild rank and a headquarters in High Rock. Their 7,000-gold loan remains to be repaid, and an important message for their official leader, Nixira Silverson, is still pending.

The Great Guild calls them to an urgent assignment for Luney IcePeak. Luney's sister has seen the crest of House Argenta stained with blood. The ancient elven family commands important maritime interests and has influence in the draconic realm; its diplomacy matters to the balance between rival powers. Its head, Lucius Argenta, has recently died, leaving uncertainty over who now commands the house.

Luney offers 1,000 gold if the party can confirm the family's leader. The three enter the Argenta mansion as IcePeak's guests, their real mission concealed.

---

## The Story

**A Death After Dinner**

Dinner offers little opportunity to investigate. The family and its other guest speak among themselves, and the visitors withdraw as rain lashes the windows. Outside, the road has become a river of mud. No one will leave the mansion that night.

A scream draws the party back to the dining room. The elderly dragonborn butler, Octavius, stands beside a young man collapsed near the table. Blood has pooled beneath the body, and an unfinished symbol is traced within it. Octavius identifies the dead man as Lucius's son and expected successor, Edrick — also called Eric during the investigation.

Tidus recognizes the mark as an incomplete seal associated with old dark elven ritual magic, though he cannot tell what it was meant to do. Nixira comforts Octavius and persuades him to sit; Tidus insists that grief does not remove the need to question the first witness.

Zacarías attempts a discreet invocation. Instead, red flames engulf him, and Octavius accuses him of the murder. Serafina Argenta, the victim's sister, arrives to find the visitors at her brother's body. The party is magically immobilized. After examining Zacarías, Serafina judges him foolish but not a murderer. She recognizes Tidus and Nixira, orders the guests out, and keeps Octavius with her. Beneath her commanding manner, the party sees a tear.

**The Doctor and the Hollow Book**

In the library, they meet Doctor Lucien Boss, an elven physician who has come to handle paperwork concerning Lucius's death. The original briefing spoke of a strange illness; Lucien says the official cause was poisoning, though he could not identify the substance and did not see the body before it was moved. Asked about the succession, he considers Serafina and Septimus possible candidates, while noting the family's reluctance to accept a woman as its head. His opinion settles nothing.

Zacarías speaks with the doctor about his unusual stature, his baldness, and the monk whose memory moved him to change his appearance. Lucien proposes a medical discussion about gnomish emotions; Zacarías finds the idea interesting but makes no commitment. The warlock also admits that the death has made him think about his own age and mortality. Lucien attributes the burning of the invocation to the mansion's ancestral defenses and recommends rest and mint water for the burns; his medical supplies are inaccessible in his carriage.

Nixira searches the library and discovers an empty hollow book. Tidus recognizes the fresh scent of the poppy flan served at dinner. The cook may have handled it recently, but whatever the book once held is unknown. Before the party moves on, Lucien gives Tidus a magical dragon-scale contact for future communication.

**Tracks, Papers, and a Portal**

In the family room, Tidus spots fresh mud on the carpet. Nixira examines the trail, and Tidus follows it alone into Lucius's study. There he finds a book on elven physiology: the passage describing a third substance capable of killing an elf has been torn out. He conceals the book. Its discussion of diet and circulation later gives him a theory about Lucius's death, but not proof.

Meanwhile, Nixira discovers two recent employment documents signed by the dead heir. One proposes dismissing Octavius with compensation and requires formalization; the other promotes the maid Elira to head butler. Elira apparently knew of her promotion, while Octavius had not been given his letter. Nixira pockets the papers and later shows them to her companions.

Zacarías finds a magical ledger recording heavy maritime losses for House Argenta, along with unpaid bills. The scale of the losses is clear; their precise duration is not. He also inspects a surprisingly cheap, hollow bust of Lucius, finding nothing inside.

Back in the study, Tidus traces the mud to a wardrobe. A drop of purple paint and a symbol inside reveal a transport portal. His later examination suggests it was recently altered for members of the Argenta family and two races he cannot identify. He waits hidden in the wardrobe, but no one arrives. He also takes a magical pen whose writing reveals a concealed truth after ten days; it has ink for roughly two uses.

**Wine and Wounds**

When Serafina retires upstairs, Nixira and Zacarías return to the dining room. Lucien is examining the body. Nixira offers the grieving Octavius company and helps him carry dishes to the kitchen. Over wine, he speaks of long service, the family's losses, and staff reductions. He suspected the younger Argentas might end his contract. He also describes Lucius and Luney's proposed commercial alliance, which could have challenged the portal mages' transport monopoly. Nixira quietly pockets a bottle of volcanic wine.

Zacarías assists Lucien and notices dried mud on the doctor's shoes. Lucien's examination raises questions about the wound, the blood, and the ritual, but his deductions are not all reliable. Mud and a physician's tools give the party reasons to ask questions, not a proven killer.

**The Vision Beneath the House**

Left with the body, Zacarías consults his grimoire and identifies the unfinished mark as a blood ritual for transferring knowledge. He completes it with the dead man's blood. The blood draws into the seal and evaporates; his eyes turn black, and a vision overtakes him.

He sees through another person's eyes: a descent to the cellar, an object apparently like a key thrown into a wine barrel, a concealed mechanism opening a stone passage, and stairs leading toward a purple glow. He cannot tell whose experience he has seen. He signals Nixira, and the party reunites to compare the vision with the portal, the letters, the losses, and their competing suspicions.

No theory accounts for everything. Tidus considers the cook and the doctor; Zacarías suspects Serafina, Lucien, and Octavius. Nixira wants to find Elira, who is reportedly upstairs. None of the accusations is established.

**A Secret Kept in Death**

Nixira uses *Speak with Dead* on the victim while her companions keep watch. The first question comes sooner than the party intended: who killed him? The answer is a refusal — **“It's a secret.”**

Asked whether his death was part of his plan, the corpse appears to answer yes. Another answer comes amid overlapping questions: **“This was to keep my house standing.”** The exchange reveals a purpose but not a killer, and the confused questions leave other details unresolved.

The party pauses its investigation with the cellar and second floor still to explore. The key, the hidden passage, the altered portal, Lucius's death, and the leadership of House Argenta remain mysteries.

---

## Key Revelations

- **The bloodstained crest has become an urgent mystery** — Lucius's son lies dead beside an incomplete ritual seal, but his killer has not been identified.
- **The ritual transfers knowledge** — Completing the seal gives Zacarías a vision of a wine barrel and a hidden descent. The vision's original owner is unknown.
- **The victim speaks of a plan to preserve his house** — His answers suggest his death was planned, but he keeps the killer's identity secret.
- **House Argenta is in financial trouble** — A ledger and bills show serious shipping losses and debts. Octavius describes staff reductions and a proposed IcePeak alliance, not a completed agreement.
- **Lucius's death remains disputed** — A briefing describes illness; Lucien reports an unidentified poison. A missing page and Tidus's theory are leads, not a solution.
- **The study conceals a modified portal** — Fresh paint and muddy tracks make it a significant clue, but neither its user nor its connection to the death is established.

---

## Consequences

- **The mission remains open** — The Argenta succession is unresolved, and Luney's 1,000-gold reward has not been paid.
- **The cellar and second floor await investigation** — The apparent key, concealed passage, purple light, and Elira's account remain unverified leads.
- **The household has reason to distrust the visitors** — Zacarías's invocation triggered the defenses and an accusation, though Serafina judged him not to be the killer.
- **The party has gathered evidence and valuables** — Nixira took the employment papers and a bottle of wine; Tidus took the physiology book and truth-revealing pen and received Lucien's contact scale.
- **The guild's obligations continue** — Its High Rock loan remains in repayment, and the Great Guild's important message to Nixira is still pending.
