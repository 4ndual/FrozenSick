# Chapter 15 — The House Gives Chase

---

## Setting the Scene

The Calvo Manco's journey through the Durst house continues in the Curse of Strahd arc. A hidden descent waits beneath the house, but Rose and Thorn are still out of reach, and the armor on the upper floor has already nearly killed Tidus. Before going down, the party chooses to finish searching the rooms around them.

---

## The Story

**The Last Page**

Nixira heals her companions and brings a flame to the dark corridor. The metal figure recoils from the light, giving the travelers room to search another bedroom. Beneath its bed they find a paper that may relate to the house's lower levels, though its meaning remains uncertain.

A locked wardrobe contains an ancient book with only one page left. The page is so fragile that touching it could destroy the words. Tidus uses a feather to brush away the dust, and the party catches its message as the page falls apart: a curse awaits those who usurp the writer's heirs, while those who help Rose and Thorn may earn a blessing. The writer fears Strahd's vengeance and hopes someone will free the trapped souls.

Nixira resolves to help the children. When she asks Eon about Rose, the little dog moves toward a door.

**Five Spirits**

A sealed door bears five symbols: a crown, a cup, a rose, a thorn and a moon. Rose explains that five spirits are needed to break the seal. The party calls on summoned companions to complete it. The door dissolves, leaving marks like burns on those involved, but the opening has a cost.

Zacarías's cobra becomes wood and flowers, forming a living cobra-shaped attachment at his stump. His familiar's bond with him has changed. Nixira's companion flowers as well, and its remaining life is shortened.

Beyond the door, the party finally speaks with Rose and Thorn. Nixira comforts Thorn and swears to protect and free him; a rose-and-thorn mark appears on her. The armored figure, called Goliath and nicknamed Alfonso, becomes an ally rather than an enemy.

**The Children's Request**

The children describe the house's terrible history and a cult beneath it. They speak of voices and souls bound together, and of other adventurers who did not escape. One young person with a faint connection to Tidus apparently got away, although the precise relationship is unclear.

Destroying the creature below will not by itself free the children. Thorn shows the party a spiritual image of a purple gem and says it must be destroyed at Strahd's altar. The children can guide the travelers but cannot enter the basement themselves; Alfonso can descend, though he grows weaker there.

The party decides to investigate. Zacarías hides inside Alfonso's great suit of armor as they venture into the darkness beneath the house. Passages light up as they move, revealing signs of a community and the tracks of many people.

**The Crypts**

Tidus finds the children's crypts. Rose and Thorn tell the party that burning their remains is the first step toward freeing them. Elsewhere, Zacarías discovers seven apparently recent bodies in red robes, drained of blood. In the cult's quarters, Nixira finds a map and notes describing a ritual: forty souls can summon a voracious creature called Strahd's astral stomach.

The party opens Rose's crypt and recovers a locket. When her remains burn, her spirit appears to suffer, then thanks them. Thorn's crypt holds poisonous thorns, but the party proceeds with the cremation. The children have taken a first step toward release; their souls are not yet free.

The travelers take red robes as disguises and approach the ritual chamber. There they see scores of bodies in positions of prayer, crumbling into gray matter. A final voice calls upon Strahd's hunger to consume the intruders. The ritual is complete, and something moves in the blood.

**Alfonso's Stand**

The party chooses escape over a fight with the summoned creature. The house's magic is now concentrated in it, and the passage back becomes a desperate chase. To give the others a head start, Alfonso stays behind to hold it off. The travelers hear metal breaking and see the creature smash the armor against the walls. Alfonso is destroyed, but his stand buys them time.

The creature catches up. Zacarías uses magic to carry the party farther away, and they force open the kitchen hatch. Behind them, the creature hurls the bones of those it has consumed. Nixira's shield, Zacarías's fire and Tidus's efforts to turn the bones aside help the party survive the barrage, though the shield breaks.

Near the exit the creature reaches them again. Zacarías avoids a final blow by sliding beneath James Buster. The horse is badly hurt but alive, and the party escapes with him. Outside, a white-haired figure appears and the travelers hear a promise that they will meet again. Amid mist and a purple dimensional effect, the house rises and moves away.

## Key Revelations

- **The sealed door demanded five spirits** — Opening it changed Zacarías's cobra and shortened the life of Nixira's companion.
- **The children are bound to the house's tragedy** — Rose and Thorn speak of a cult, trapped souls and a purple gem that must be destroyed at Strahd's altar.
- **The ritual needed forty souls** — The cult's notes describe a rite to summon Strahd's voracious astral stomach. The party arrives too late to stop it.
- **Cremation is only a beginning** — Burning the children's remains begins their release and removes the curse associated with carrying the property papers away, but does not complete their liberation.
- **Alfonso buys the party time** — The animated armor holds the creature back and is destroyed.

---

## Consequences

- **The Calvo Manco escapes** — Tidus, Nixira and Zacarías survive, as does the wounded James Buster. The creature is not shown defeated.
- **The children remain bound** — The purple gem and Strahd's altar remain part of the task Nixira has promised to pursue.
- **The seal leaves lasting changes** — Zacarías's cobra is now attached to his stump in wooden form, and Nixira's companion has a shortened life.
- **Alfonso is lost** — The armor is broken during the chase; the fate of whatever animated it is not established.
- **The house is gone from immediate reach** — It moves away through mist after the escape. The identity of the white-haired figure is unconfirmed.
