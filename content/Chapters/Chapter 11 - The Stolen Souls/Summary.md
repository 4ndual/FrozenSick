# Chapter 11 — The Stolen Souls

---

## Setting the Scene

The Calvo Manco remain guests in House Argenta's mansion, trapped by rain and surrounded by unanswered questions. Lucius Argenta is dead, and his son Eric has been found murdered beside an unfinished blood seal. The party's task for Luney IcePeak is to discover who leads the influential family, but the succession remains uncertain.

Zacarías's ritual vision pointed toward a key hidden in wine and a concealed passage. Upstairs, the maid Elira and the other guests may know more than they have told.

---

## The Story

**The Maid and the Widow**

The party finds Elira preparing the guest rooms. She is grieving Eric, and Nixira offers a sympathetic ear, playing a mournful tune as they talk. Elsewhere upstairs, Nixira and Zacarías discover the belongings of Elena, a widow whose husband had been close to Lucius and died at sea.

Tidus meets Elena, and Zacarías later joins her, seeking information during an intimate encounter. He also takes eight small rubies from her clothing. Elena speaks of insurance on lost ships and a forthcoming family council. She suspects the Great Guild had a hand in Lucius's death and speculates about the Argenta siblings, but her theories are not proof.

Elira shows Nixira the ancestral portraits: the heirs depicted are men, and the council may decide between Serafina and Septimus. When Elira becomes ill, Tidus and Nixira realize she is pregnant. She fears what will happen if the household discovers it. Tidus offers to help, and Nixira promises discretion.

A touch reveals something more disturbing. Elira is under a controlling enchantment, though contact briefly allows her to speak more freely. Frightened and uncertain where she could flee, she appears to accuse Septimus of Eric's murder. Her accusation gives the party a lead, not a proven account of the killing.

**A Familiar Face**

The investigators compare theories and study the portraits, noticing a growing resemblance among recent ancestors. Their explanations remain guesses. Nixira then visits Septimus with wine; conversation turns intimate. Tidus and Zacarías, worried when she does not return as agreed, burst into the room. Their attempted rescue infuriates Nixira, and Septimus demonstrates his magical strength by restraining the intruders.

The confrontation gives way to conversation. Tidus recognizes Septimus as a man who once helped protect him after an affair with an elven princess. Septimus presents himself as a diplomat with little interest in leading the family. Tidus and Zacarías have found signed insurance authorizations among Elena's papers, and the group discusses the family's losses and succession with him.

Tidus also tells Septimus about Elira's pregnancy, despite Nixira's concern that the information could put her in danger. A mark associated with the revolutionary network helps win Tidus's trust. After the party leaves, Septimus opens a private magical communication.

**The Key in the Barrel**

With Octavius's help, the party turns to the cellar. Zacarías spots a key in a barrel, and Tidus retrieves it from liquid that damages his clothing. A concealed lever reveals a runed stone door protected by magic.

A nearby grimoire describes a forbidden soul-transmutation ritual requiring four souls and a limited window of time. The party recalls what Eric's corpse said about a transmutation meant to keep the family standing. The vision has led them to a real hiding place, but the ritual's purpose and Eric's full plan remain uncertain.

**The Portraits Behind the Portraits**

Serafina accompanies the investigators to the portrait room. Septimus closes the door, separating Zacarías from Tidus and Nixira; Zacarías sees him carrying Elira unconscious and gagged. Serafina initially suspects the visitors, then agrees to cooperate as the danger becomes clearer.

She speaks of Eric's role in the burning of ships and reveals what the family has concealed in its ancestral portraits: signs of deterioration associated with their consumption of meat. Lucius's portrait is different, with a small blue crystal visible at his neck. Its meaning is unclear.

A powerful magical disturbance shakes the mansion. Nixira tells Serafina what they learned about Elira's enchantment, pregnancy, and the transmutation. Octavius reveals that he had been feigning drunkenness at Serafina's request. Together they hurry toward the hidden chamber.

**The Ritual Chamber**

Inside, a dark figure conducts a ritual. Zacarías regains consciousness in magical restraints, and Elira hangs unconscious nearby. Serafina attacks Septimus; Octavius confronts Doctor Lucien Boss and Elena. Tidus shoots Lucien, killing him, but the doctor's body is drawn toward the ritual.

Tidus raises his soul-containing medallion, hoping to take the doctor's soul. Instead, three souls are drawn from the medallion. Darkness engulfs the party. Bhaal tells them he did not surrender the souls willingly, urges them to escape, and directs them to seek a temple on the Sword Coast.

The Calvo Manco flee without witnessing the final outcome of the fight or the ritual.

**A Regent and Three Fugitives**

During the journey back, the party learns that their gods' influence over DragonLand is failing: they can no longer call on them there as before. News names Septimus the official regent and reports that Serafina died by suicide after her brother's murder. The party did not witness her death, and the reported circumstances remain unverified. A magical image shows Septimus with Elira, described as his wife and visibly pregnant; she appears to remain enchanted. Warrants are circulating for all three adventurers.

Septimus is revealed as a double agent. The party reports to Luney, who recognizes the danger to the revolutionary network but cannot yet know the extent of the compromise. Luney advises keeping the family's meat secret and the child's parentage from the guild. A reward is discussed, though the amounts and allocation are unclear.

Luney also tells Tidus that he has three children. The party considers seeking them, alongside other dangerous possibilities, but its next journey is not settled.

## Key Revelations

- **Elira is pregnant and magically controlled** — Briefly able to speak more freely, she fears discovery and apparently implicates Septimus in Eric's death. Her accusation is not independent proof.
- **The cellar vision was a genuine lead** — The key and hidden lever exist; a grimoire nearby describes a four-soul transmutation ritual.
- **House Argenta has a concealed history** — Serafina reveals altered portraits and deterioration associated with the family's meat consumption. Lucius's blue crystal remains unexplained.
- **Septimus is a double agent** — His familiarity with Tidus and revolutionary mark helped him gain trust before the confrontation at the ritual.
- **The ritual took three souls from Tidus's medallion** — Bhaal denies giving them up willingly. The ritual's ultimate outcome and the power behind it remain uncertain.
- **The public account favors Septimus** — He is named regent; Serafina's reported suicide is not witnessed by the party.

---

## Consequences

- **The party is wanted** — Warrants circulate for Tidus, Nixira, and Zacarías after their escape.
- **Elira's safety remains uncertain** — News presents her as Septimus's pregnant wife, apparently still enchanted; her consent and the child's fate are not established.
- **Divine contact is disrupted on DragonLand** — The party's gods can no longer be contacted there as before. Bhaal has directed them toward a Sword Coast temple.
- **The revolutionary network faces a breach** — Luney knows Septimus was a double agent, but the reach of the betrayal is unknown.
- **The Argenta mysteries remain open** — Eric's killer, the ritual's ultimate result, and the immediate fates of Octavius and Elena are unresolved. Lucien was killed by Tidus, though later public news describes him as missing.
- **A new family lead awaits Tidus** — Luney says he has three children; the party discusses finding them without completing the journey.
