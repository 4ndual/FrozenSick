# Chapter 12 — The House in the Mist

---

## Setting the Scene

Wanted after the disaster at House Argenta, the founders of the Calvo Manco prepare to leave DragonLand. Luney IcePeak has warned them that their enemies mean to kill them, and Tidus has learned that he has three children. A desert route, a port, and a voyage toward the IcePeak family are discussed, but the road has other plans.

The session begins a Curse of Strahd arc: a search through unnatural mist leads the party to the Durst house. Whether the missing travelers have reached it, and what waits beneath it, remain unanswered.

---

## The Story

**Leaving La Ultima Gota**

At the tavern, the party weighs what it can carry and whom it can trust. Nixira speaks of freeing herself from infernal control and eventually killing her father. Zacarías wants to reconnect with his gnomish roots; Tidus hopes the guild will grow. The wyvern egg and guild possessions are among the things discussed for safekeeping.

An alarm cuts the preparations short. Tidus activates a portal stone, and the exhausted travelers arrive at a rural house outside Brasboredon. Their intended journey has scarcely begun.

**Agatha's Last Sunrise**

Inside they find Agatha, the mother of Vermilion and Dorotea, gravely ill and struggling to breathe. Nixira helps make her comfortable. Agatha says her daughters went out for provisions and have not returned; the party understands that they have been missing for nineteen days.

Outside, Zacarías finds an injured horse, James Buster, still bearing damaged harness. With magic he speaks to the animal, whose fragmented account suggests that fog and foul-smelling attackers overtook the carriage and that Dorotea cut the horses free. His raven searches as well, but neither sister is found.

Desperate to help Agatha, Zacarías suggests that Tidus bite her. Tidus refuses to do so without her consent. The argument grows heated, and Tidus punches him. Later, Zacarías apologizes for his hurtful words and agrees to keep a personal confidence, except in a matter of life and death.

The party cannot cure Agatha. Instead, they create the illusion of a sunrise for her, while Nixira helps dress her and ease her pain. Agatha knows the sunrise is not real, but thanks them. She asks that a letter reach her daughter, and dies peacefully.

**The Fog and the Road**

A vision offers Tidus unsettling fragments: gray figures, a sapphire associated with Vermilion, and a carriage marked with a circle and three dots like an emblem connected to his son. These are clues, not an account of what happened to the missing women. Zacarías detects dark magic, though its connection to the fog is uncertain.

Tidus rides Buster into the mist. The journey seems far longer to him than it does to those watching, and Zacarías uses his raven to help guide the horse back. Nixira briefly drives the fog away with radiant power, only for it to return. The party rests. By morning the rain is heavy, and the travelers must decide where to go next.

Wanted notices near the city remind them of the danger awaiting them there. Zacarías changes his appearance with an illusion. The group turns instead toward the trail of the missing women, with Buster guiding them into the woods.

**The Wreck and the Letter**

In the forest, Nixira notices a blood-marked tree bearing the circle and three dots. Beyond it lie broken trees, burned wreckage, and ruined supplies. A lichen, questioned through magic, tells of travelers lost in the mist and attacked by pale, flesh-biting beings. According to its account, some fled into the fog together. The witness also tries to entangle Nixira, and its story cannot be taken as certain proof of the sisters' fate.

A concealed black box holds a marked scarf that Tidus recognizes as connected to his son, a white feather that reminds Nixira of Agatha, and an old amber fragment marked with indistinct runes. Nixira takes the feather; Zacarías takes the amber.

Tidus opens the letter meant for Vermilion and Dorotea. Their mother's words urge them to keep living and not let small wounds divide them after her death. The party still has no one to deliver it to.

As the mist closes in, the amber pulls upward. Wind carries away the feather and scarf, but Zacarías's raven catches the feather and Tidus catches the scarf. The road seems to shorten, and the party suddenly finds itself at the house ahead. What caused the change is unclear.

**The Durst House**

The isolated white house rises four stories above them. Tidus sees a small handprint at an upper window. Zacarías coaxes the reluctant Buster onto a sheltered porch, where the horse remains while the party enters. Beyond the inner door, the cold is intense.

A portrait depicts the Durst family, including children named Rose and Thorn. A clock shows six in the evening, although the travelers believe it should be morning. The clock and portrait exchange places, and a warning writes itself in blood: a beast beneath the house will wake at midnight, and a gift is needed to appease it. The message warns of servants, and doors open throughout the house.

The ground floor is full of contradictions. A dining table is set beneath dust. The kitchen holds spices, a sharp blade, a warm oven, and a small service lift. Recently cut damp wood lies in a house that otherwise seems neglected. Hungry rats sound within the walls, and small footsteps move on the stairs.

At a window Nixira sees a purple tentacle with suckers; more appear outside. Tidus finds a heavy hunting crossbow with twenty silver bolts, and a tarot deck he recognizes as Vermilion's despite its changed illustrations. He keeps the deck. When the party checks on Buster, the horse refuses to come inside but says he is all right on the porch. Zacarías summons a hare to keep him company and warn them of trouble.

Before going upstairs, the party notices hand or claw marks and shoe prints on the ceiling, as if something had crawled overhead. The search pauses with the sisters still missing and the upper floors unexplored.

## Key Revelations

- **Vermilion and Dorotea are missing** — Agatha says they left for provisions and have not returned. Buster's account and the wreck offer leads, not proof of their present whereabouts.
- **The mist distorts the journey** — Tidus's experience of time differs from that of the others watching him; the road later seems to shorten near the house. The cause is unknown.
- **The emblem returns** — A circle and three dots appear in Tidus's vision and on a blood-marked tree near the wreck. The connection to his son remains unexplained.
- **The lichen's story is uncertain** — It describes an attack by pale beings, but also tries to trap Nixira. Its testimony is not independently confirmed.
- **The Durst house issues a warning** — A bloody message claims a beast beneath the house wakes at midnight and demands a gift. The party has not verified the claim.
- **Vermilion's deck is in the house** — Tidus recognizes the tarot deck, though its illustrations have changed. Its presence does not establish where she is.

---

## Consequences

- **Agatha is dead** — The party gave her comfort at the end. Her letter remains undelivered.
- **The search continues** — The sisters and the other travelers described at the wreck have not been found.
- **The Calvo Manco remain wanted** — Notices near the city underline the risk of returning openly.
- **The party is inside the Durst house** — Its upper floors and the alleged creature below remain unexplored. Buster and the summoned hare are on the porch.
- **New clues are in the party's hands** — The scarf, feather, amber fragment, letter, and tarot deck may matter to the search, but their full significance is unknown.
