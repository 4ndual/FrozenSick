# Active Quests

Only mission-level objectives still in progress belong here.

## 1. Destroy the Golden Crown (Corona Dorada)

- **Objective:** Find and destroy the mind-control artifact wielded by Malacor.
- **Alliance:** Dracus agreed to help because the Crown threatens everyone.
- **Lead:** Luney told Dracus there is a path to the Crown's destruction, though the method is still unknown.
- **Status:** No confirmed progress since the ambush.
- **Stakes:** As long as the Crown exists, Malacor can force obedience with a single command.

## 2. Find Vermilion and Dorotea

- **Objective:** Follow the missing sisters' trail and learn what happened to them.
- **Lead:** Agatha reported them missing; Buster described an attack, and a wreck led the party through the mist to the Durst house. Vermilion's tarot deck was found inside.
- **Status:** Both remain missing; the house's upper floors are unexplored.

## 3. Seek Bhaal's Temple

- **Objective:** Seek a temple on the Sword Coast as Bhaal instructed after three souls were taken from Tidus's medallion.
- **Status:** The party escaped the Argenta ritual; the journey to the temple is not shown.
