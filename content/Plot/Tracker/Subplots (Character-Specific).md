- **Three children:** Luney told him he has three children. The party discussed seeking them, but no journey to find them has been completed.
- **Stolen souls:** Three souls were taken from his medallion during the Argenta ritual; Bhaal directed the party toward a Sword Coast temple.

## Nixira

## Zacarias

- **Gnomish roots:** He wants to reconnect with them. Lucien proposed a discussion about gnomish emotions and possible opposition to enslavement; Zacarias was willing to discuss it but did not accept.
- **Mortality:** The Argenta death prompted him to reflect on his own age and mortality.
