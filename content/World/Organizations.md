# Organizations - Frozen Sick

---

This page now treats each organization as its own entity and separates the Five Great Houses into a nested political structure instead of a single mega-organization.

## Major Orders and Factions

### La ultima Gota

**Type:** Tavern front / covert refuge  
**Leader:** Luney IcePeak  
**Base:** The Dragon Scar

La ultima Gota is the party's safest known refuge and one of the clearest operational hubs in the setting. It is both sanctuary and trap-ready stronghold.

### Dragonforce

**Type:** Military machine of DragonLand  
**Command center:** DragonBorn

Dragonforce enforces crown power and increasingly reflects Dranlek's brutality.

### El Velo de las Siete Canciones

**Type:** Musical order turned covert network  
**Legacy alias:** Las Cuerdas Rotas

These names refer to the same organization. The canonical name across the project is **El Velo de las Siete Canciones**. It began as a healing order and became a political instrument with agents, handlers, and compromised loyalties.

### La Piedra Sangrante

**Type:** Heraldic order  
**Status:** Underdefined but symbolically important

Its insignia appears on **Kahel Varos "El Heraldo"**, which keeps it politically relevant even if its hierarchy remains obscure.

### Centinelas de la Era Blanca

**Type:** Intelligence and sentinel network  
**Known operative:** Kahel Varos "El Heraldo"

The organization moves information quickly and appears wherever older loyalties and field intelligence still matter.

### Orden del Silencio

**Type:** Monastic order  
**Known figures:** Senor Nadie, Fuercitas, Fresitas, Rodrigo

A discipline-driven order with a major impact on Chapter 3 onward.

## The Five Great Houses of DragonLand

This is a political family bloc, not a single organization. Each house below stands alone.

### Casa Gremmory

**Element:** Fire  
**Domain:** Noctyra

Commercial, political, and coercive influence concentrated in the fire-aligned regions.

**Known figures:** Tywin Gremmory, Torin Gremmory, Vermy Gremmory

### Casa IcePeak

**Element:** Ice  
**Domain:** Valdrenor

The house most tied to the party through Luney, Donner, and the wider WinterSpeak line.

### DragonHeart

**Element:** Arcana  
**Domain:** Kharvok

Arcane nobility aligned with the kingdom's central power structure.

### Casa DeepWater

**Element:** Sea / Rivers  
**Domain:** Draknor

River and maritime influence with logistical relevance across the island.

### Casa Draksky

**Element:** Air / Wind  
**Domain:** Confederated territories

A looser union of minor lords. Farquad belongs to this house, but his personal troops are not treated here as a separate formal organization.

## Canon Decisions Applied

- **El Velo de las Siete Canciones** is the canonical name. `Las Cuerdas Rotas` survives only as a legacy alias.
- **Farquad's Forces** was removed as a formal organization. It remains valid only as a descriptive phrase for troops loyal to Farquad.
- **Malacor's Network** was removed as a formal organization. References now treat it as Malacor's operatives, circle, or influence rather than an institution.

## Chapters 10–12 — Organizations and Updates

### House Argenta

**Type:** Elven maritime family  
**Status:** Septimus publicly named regent after a disputed succession

An ancient family with shipping interests and diplomatic influence in the draconic realm. Its ledger shows heavy maritime losses and debts. Octavius described a proposed commercial alliance between Lucius and Casa IcePeak that could have challenged the portal mages' transport monopoly; no completed pact is established. Septimus is a confirmed double agent, but the reach of the revolutionary network's compromise is unknown.

### The Portal Mages' Guild

**Type:** Transport organization  
**Status:** Discussed, not directly encountered

Its transport monopoly was discussed in connection with the proposed Argenta–IcePeak alliance.

### The Calvo Manco Guild

**Type:** Adventuring guild  
**Status:** Founders wanted

After reporting the Argenta affair to Luney, the founders diverted from their intended journey to search for Vermilion and Dorotea. They entered the Durst house through the mist.

## Chapters 13–14 — Organization and Family Updates

### The Calvo Manco Guild

The founders continued searching the Durst house for Vermilion and Dorotea. They opened the barrier to a forbidden library and reached a concealed upper floor, but found neither sister and have not freed the children who offered help in return for release.

### The Durst Family

**Type:** Family associated with the house and a mill  
**Status:** Circumstances remain uncertain

The family portrait and will name Rose and Thorn. The will conditionally leaves them the house, mill, and other family property if their parents die; it does not transfer property to the party. A letter by Strahd von Zarovich alleges that its addressee and associates practiced sacrifice and tortured visitors, but the party has not independently verified those accusations.

## Chapter 15 — Organizations and Updates

### The House's Cult

**Type:** Cult associated with the Durst house's basement  
**Status:** Surviving membership unknown

Rose and Thorn spoke of a cult beneath the house. Its quarters, ritual notes and seven apparently recent blood-drained bodies in red robes provide evidence of the group. The notes describe a forty-soul ritual to summon a creature called Strahd's astral stomach; the summoning was completed before the party escaped.

### The Calvo Manco Guild

The founders investigated the basement and escaped the summoned creature with James Buster. Nixira promised to protect and free Thorn; the children's release remains incomplete.
